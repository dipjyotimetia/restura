Built for people who don’t want their tokens on someone else’s server.

Nothing leaves your machine

No account. Collections, history and environments live in IndexedDB or your OS keychain. Crash reports are opt-out and scrubbed of request content.

Scripts that can’t escape

Pre-request and test scripts run in a QuickJS WebAssembly sandbox — no DOM, filesystem or network, with memory and time caps. Postman’s pm.* API works.

Chain requests into workflows

Typed bindings, guards, loops and try/catch. Run them in the app, or in CI with restura workflow run.
